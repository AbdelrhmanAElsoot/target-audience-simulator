# Product Specification: Craftly — FICTIONAL SAMPLE

> **This brief is a fictional sample written for testing the skill.** "Craftly" is an invented brand; the course, prices and audience below are imaginary and any resemblance to a real brand or business is coincidental. Replace every number with your real ones and send your own brief.

## 1. Core Concept
* **Brand Name:** Craftly — fictional
* **Value Proposition:** A 6-week live online course, "Freelance Graphic Design from Zero", that takes a complete beginner to a paid first client.
* **Key Features:** 18 live sessions (3 per week) on Zoom, session recordings kept forever, weekly critique of real briefs, private community (Discord + WhatsApp group), a completion certificate, and a final portfolio project reviewed by working designers.
* **Prices (real numbers, not tiers):** 1,400 EGP for the full cohort; installment plan 2 × 750 EGP; alumni retake 400 EGP; free 90-minute intro workshop.
* **Delivery:** cohort-based via Zoom; next cohort capped at 40 seats.
* **Stage:** pre-launch — Instagram at ~3,000 followers, a free intro workshop done once with 85 attendees, no paid cohort sold yet.

## 2. Target Audience & Personas to Simulate

1. **The Recent Graduate:**
   - Context: Finished a non-design degree, unemployed or in a first low-paid job, wants a sellable skill fast, budget ~1,000-2,000 EGP total for self-education.
   - Bias: Skeptical of "learn design in 6 weeks" promises, worries about getting actual feedback instead of passive videos.

2. **The Side-Hustler Employee:**
   - Context: Full-time employee (28-35) who wants freelance income on the side; can attend evening sessions only; comfortable paying for quality.
   - Bias: Compares against YouTube and cheaper recorded courses; wants proof the first client is realistic.

3. **The Experienced Self-Learner / Skeptic:**
   - Context: Already followed free tutorials, has a small portfolio, considers structured education unnecessary; will scrutinize curriculum, instructor credentials and price per session.
   - Bias: Very critical; questions instructor track record, cohort size, refund policy and what "certificate" is worth.

## 3. Simulation Goal
Simulate a 25-to-30 post X (Twitter) feed reacting to the launch announcement of Craftly's first paid cohort. Focus on honest objections: the price versus recorded-course alternatives, whether live attendance is realistic for full-time employees, instructor credibility, and the value of the certificate.
